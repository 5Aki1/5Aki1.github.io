I'm a computational biologist and PhD candidate in Human Genetics at McGill. I build tools and pipelines that find and interpret functional noncoding variants in cancer, using large-scale sequencing and epigenomic data.

I work in the [Bailey Lab](https://github.com/SvenBaileyLab), where I study noncoding regulatory mutations in esophageal and gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma. I graduate in early 2027.

## What I work on

- **Variant effect prediction.** Predicting how noncoding variants change transcription factor binding, directly from ChIP-seq signal. See [[software/eubar|EUbar]].
- **Chromatin structure.** Finding nucleosome-free regions inside histone modification peaks. See [[software/histrader|HisTrader]].
- **Noncoding drivers in cancer.** Linking regulatory elements to genes with HiChIP and testing them for mutational burden in whole-genome cohorts.
- **Pipelines.** Nextflow pipelines for reproducible processing and integration of epigenomic data (ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq, DNase-seq, and HiChIP).
