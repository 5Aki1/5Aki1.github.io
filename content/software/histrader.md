
HisTrader finds nucleosome-free regions (NFRs) inside histone modification peaks from ChIP-seq signal (bedGraph or bigWig).

- Code: [github.com/SvenBaileyLab/HisTrader](https://github.com/SvenBaileyLab/HisTrader)
- Paper: [[publications|*Cell Reports Methods* (2026)]]
